# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Factory operators, coating store technicians, and warehouse supervisors. They use the tool on the factory floor (often on mobile devices or tablets) under varying ambient light conditions to quickly withdraw (เบิกของ), receive (รับของ), check stock levels, and scan QR codes.

## Product Purpose
The AGC Coating Store System is designed to manage spare parts inventory efficiently. Its primary goal is to ensure fast, reliable, and error-free tracking of parts intake, withdrawals, and stock counts.

## Brand Personality
- Voice & Tone: Clear, direct, professional, and reliable.
- 3-Word Personality: Industrial, Utility-focused, High-contrast.
- Emotional Goals: Instill confidence, reduce operational errors, and provide immediate clarity. Uses AGC corporate blue (`#001F6B`) and red (`#E40046`) for identity.

## Anti-references
- SaaS Clichés: Avoid cream/bone backgrounds, thin/soft wide drop shadows on borders ("ghost cards"), tiny all-caps section eyebrows, text gradients, and decorative glassmorphism.
- Non-functional UI: No low-contrast text or decorative elements that hinder scanning and reading in bright or dusty factory settings.

## Design Principles
1. **Utility Over Decoration**: Every visual element must serve a functional purpose. Navigation, status badges, and actions should be clear and immediate.
2. **High-Contrast Clarity**: High visibility under any light condition. Text contrast must exceed WCAG AA standards.
3. **Ergonomic Touch Targets**: Large buttons and controls optimized for hand-held scanning and fast actions on mobile.
4. **Action-Differentiated Colors**: Use distinct, recognizable color codes for critical actions (e.g., Withdraw vs. Receive) to prevent human errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for standard text.
- Large and readable typography for ease of use under physical fatigue.
- Support for keyboard-friendly and touch-friendly controls.
